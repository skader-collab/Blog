Phishing Awareness

Stay Informed. Stay Secure.

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack that uses disguised email as a weapon. The goal is to trick the email recipient into believing that the message is something they want or need — a request from their bank, for instance, or a note from someone in their company — and to click a link or download an attachment.

Common Techniques

  • Email Phishing: Fraudulent emails from reputable sources.
  • Spear Phishing: Targeted attacks at specific individuals.
  • Whaling: Attacks directed at high-profile targets.
  • Smishing/Vishing: Attacks via SMS or voice calls.

How to Protect Yourself

  • Be cautious of unsolicited messages.
  • Verify sender email addresses.
  • Avoid unknown links or attachments.
  • Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
  • Keep software and antivirus updated.

Suspect a Phishing Attempt?

If you receive a suspicious email or message, do not respond or click any links. Report it to your IT department or email provider immediately. If you believe you have fallen victim to a phishing attack, change your passwords and monitor your accounts for any suspicious activity or unauthorized transactions.
